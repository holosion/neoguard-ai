A prototype that watches temperature, SpO₂ and heart rate for possible hypothermia and hypoxia. It alerts locally, stores data offline, synchronises later and adds explainable AI as an extra layer of attention.

01 · Why it matters

Danger can arrive between checks.

Hypothermia and hypoxia can worsen quickly in newborns, and in low-resource wards staff, power and internet are not always reliable.

02 · Sensor: MAX30102

Light reads the pulse.

  • MAX30102 — heart rate and SpO₂ over I²C.

Red and infrared light shine into the fingertip, and the sensor reads how the returned light changes with every heartbeat.

02 · Sensor: MLX90614

Temperature without touching.

  • MLX90614 — non-contact temperature over the same bus.

Infrared energy from the skin is measured from a distance, so the baby is not disturbed.

02 · Hardware layer

Every signal has a path.

  • MAX30102 — heart rate and SpO₂ over I²C.
  • MLX90614 — non-contact temperature over the same bus.
  • DS1302 — timekeeping for trustworthy timestamps.
  • ST7789 + SD — wearable-style display and offline storage.
  • RGB + buzzer — immediate human-readable alarms.
03 · Firmware

The safety path never waits for AI.

The ESP32 samples the sensors, validates readings, timestamps them and applies local threshold rules.

  • Normal: show values and continue sampling.
  • Abnormal: drive the LED, buzzer and display locally.
  • Offline: save readings to the SD card.
  • Online again: synchronise queued data in order.

05 · AI integration

AI sees the trend, not just the alarm.

The backend model looks at a causal 120-second history and, for temperature, SpO₂ and heart rate, calculates the last value, mean, slope and variation.

  • Features: 12 explainable time-series features.
  • Model: logistic regression with a validation-selected threshold of 0.75.
  • Output: risk score, contributing factors and trend flags.
  • Boundary: AI is advisory; local ESP32 alerts remain independent.
06 · What the evaluation showed

Evidence before promises.

0.9748

ROC-AUC on the synthetic held-out test set. The model detected 44 of 49 simulated events at onset with a median lead time of 107.5 seconds.

Firmware upload, SD diagnostics and HTTP runtime/heartbeat/synchronisation checks passed. Clinical accuracy was not established.

07 · The human outcome

Earlier attention. Safer decisions.

NeoGuard does not replace nurses or doctors. It gives them a persistent local signal, preserved history and an explainable second layer of attention.

Our next step is calibrated reference testing, ethical clinical evaluation, power qualification and validation with real neonatal outcomes.
